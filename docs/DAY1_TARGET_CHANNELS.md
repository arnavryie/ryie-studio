# Day 1 Action Channels — Where to Hunt Clients, Bounties & Paid Gigs Today

Zero-cost platforms where you can apply, pitch, or ship for real payouts immediately.

---

## 1. Local Pune Demo-First Outreach (Fastest Cash: ₹12,000–₹25,000/client)

Open **Google Maps** and search these exact categories in Pune (`Baner`, `Wakad`, `Hinjewadi`, `Kothrud`, `Koregaon Park`, `Viman Nagar`, `Pimpri`):

- `dental clinic near Baner` / `physiotherapy clinic Wakad`
- `gym near Kothrud` / `fitness studio Viman Nagar`
- `specialty cafe Koregaon Park` / `cloud kitchen Wakad`

**Pick businesses with:**
- 4.2★+ ratings and 50+ reviews (they care about reputation and have revenue)
- **NO website link**, a broken `http://` link, or a slow Wix/Linktree page

**Action:** Open `/pitch`, enter their name + neighborhood, copy their custom live preview link, and send via WhatsApp / Instagram DM / email.

## 2. International Freelance & Founder Gigs (\$350–\$750/build)

- **X (Twitter) Search Queries (filter by "Latest"):**
  - `"looking for" "next.js" OR "landing page" OR "mvp"`
  - `"need a developer" "ai" OR "saas" OR "website"`
  - `"#buildinpublic" "landing page"` (find founders with rough launch pages and DM a custom `/demo/saas?brand=...` link)
- **Reddit:**
  - `https://www.reddit.com/r/forhire/` (post a `[For Hire]` thread + reply fast to `[Hiring]` web/AI posts)
  - `https://www.reddit.com/r/freelance_forhire/`
  - `https://www.reddit.com/r/slavelabour/` (good for fast \$50–\$150 quick scripts/landing pages to build initial momentum)
- **Freelance Marketplaces:**
  - **Contra** (`contra.com`) — zero commission on freelancer payouts, great for modern React/AI portfolios
  - **Upwork** (`upwork.com`) — filter jobs `< 5 proposals`, posted `< 1 hr ago`, attach 2 relevant live links (`agentearn-ai.vercel.app`, `lunar-statio.vercel.app`, `clinic-sigma-eosin.vercel.app`)
