## Detailed Findings & Traps to Avoid

### 1. YouTube Shorts & Vertical Live Streaming in India
- **Official YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Tiers:**
  - **500 subscribers + 3M Shorts views (90d) or 3,000 watch hours (12m):** unlocks **Fan Funding only** (Super Chat, Memberships, Shopping) — **no AdSense ad revenue**.
  - **1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views (90d) or 4,000 watch hours (12m):** unlocks AdSense revenue sharing.
- **India Shorts RPM Reality (Reddit creator consensus):** **₹3–₹12 per 1,000 views** (roughly \$0.01–\$0.05/1K). Even **1M views** in India typically earns **₹3,000–₹12,000**.
- **Vertical 9:16 Live Streaming:** Pushes streams straight into the YouTube Shorts feed for rapid subscriber and VOD watch-hour growth.
- **Critical Bank Safety Rule:** Putting a personal bank UPI QR directly on screen during a public stream carries serious **Indian Cyber Cell bank-freeze risk** if even one viewer pays from a compromised/fraudulent account. Always use a separate merchant/intermediary tip tool (e.g., StreamAlert / Razorpay-backed alert overlay) rather than your primary bank UPI.

### 2. Clipping Campaigns (MusubiClip, IndClip, Whop)
- **Model is legitimate**, not an outright scam — brands escrow budget and pay per 1,000 verified Reels/Shorts views.
- **MusubiClip:** India-registered, **direct UPI** withdrawal (₹10 min, 5% fee, ~48h payout), campaign CPMs typically start around ₹10–₹100+ per 1K verified views.
- **IndClip:** India-focused, campaign briefs often range ₹100–₹250+ per 1K verified YouTube views (with campaign minimums/caps and manual post-campaign settlement).
- **Whop Content Rewards:** Does **not** support UPI; requires cross-border Stripe/PayPal/KYC.
- **Reality:** View audits reject bots/low-retention traffic, and fresh IG/YT pages can get throttled. Treat it as **₹5k–₹20k side stack**, not your main ₹80k bet.

### 3. UGC & Creator Campaigns in India
- **Avoid wasting time on US-centric physical-shipping apps:** `Billo` is geo-limited for India; `SideShift` heavily prioritizes US/UK creators; avoid obscure task apps (`Laiza.live`-style sites with shifting domains) and never pay any "activation fee."
- **What actually converts in India:**
  - **WYLD:** India-first D2C nano-creator app.
  - **Collabstr + Direct Cold Pitching to AI/SaaS Startups:** Software & AI apps don't require physical shipping. Standard Indian 30–45s UGC video pricing ranges ₹5,000–₹25,000 (beginners often close ₹2,000–₹6,000/video).

### 4. AI Coding Training (High-Upside Zero-Bidding Option)
- **Outlier AI:** Recruits Indian coders; weekly Tuesday payouts (PayPal/direct), but requires strict ID/biometric verification and has frequent Empty Queue (EQ) pauses.
- **DataAnnotation.tech:** High coding rates globally, strict silent vetting from India.
- **Mercor:** AI interview matching to engineering/AI contracts.
- **Move:** Spend 60 minutes applying to all three once; keep tasking if accepted.
