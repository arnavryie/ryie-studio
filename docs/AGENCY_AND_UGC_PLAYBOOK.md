# Agency White-Label + SaaS UGC Playbook (Zero Upwork / Zero Fiverr)

---

## 1. Finding Boutique Dev & Design Agencies (10–30 Staff)

One overflow retainer (`$18–$25/hr` or `$400–$700` per sprint) = **₹50,000–₹80,000/month** without chasing dozens of small gigs.

### Search Queries (LinkedIn & Google)
- LinkedIn People / Companies (`11–50 employees`, `United States` / `United Kingdom` / `UAE` / `Australia` / `Bengaluru`):
  - `"Founder" AND ("Webflow agency" OR "Next.js agency" OR "AI automation agency")`
  - `"Creative Director" AND "digital agency"`
  - `"CTO" AND ("software studio" OR "product studio")`
- Google Maps / Clutch / GoodFirms:
  - `"digital product agency Austin"`, `"Next.js agency London"`, `"AI automation studio Dubai"`

### 60-Second Loom Video Teardown Script (Highest Reply Rate)
1. **0–10s:** Open their site or a client case study on screen: *"Hey [Name], quick 60-sec clip — I build Next.js/FastAPI + AI apps in Pune and checked out [Agency]."*
2. **10–40s:** Point out ONE real improvement (mobile load, interactive demo preview, or AI lead desk) and pull up `https://ryie-studio.vercel.app` + `https://agentearn-ai.vercel.app`.
3. **40–60s:** *"Whenever your core devs are slammed with client deadlines, I take white-label Next.js or AI chatbot builds off your plate in 3–7 days. Happy to clear one small ticket as a paid trial this week."*

## 2. Developer-Led SaaS / AI Product UGC (`https://ryie-studio.vercel.app/ugc`)

Software startups **don't need physical shipping** to India and pay **$75–$190 (₹3,500–₹9,000+)** for crisp 30–45s vertical walkthroughs for X, Reels, and paid ads.

### 3-Step Recording Setup (Using Your Laptop + OBS + iPad Air 4)
1. **Canvas:** `1080×1920` (9:16 vertical) in OBS — top 70% crisp browser zoom of the product UI, bottom/corner clean webcam crop with your tuned mic.
2. **Hook Structure (First 3 Seconds):** *"Stop doing [manual task] in [spreadsheet/tool] — watch how [App] automates it in 15 seconds."*
3. **Where to Pitch:**
   - X (`#buildinpublic`, `#indiehackers`, recent Product Hunt launches) using `/pitch` → `AI SaaS UGC Deal` preset
   - **Collabstr** (`collabstr.com`) profile positioned strictly around *"Tech, AI & SaaS Product Demo Reels"*
   - **WYLD** (`getwyld.in`) for Indian D2C brand briefs

---

## 3. Safe Indian Clipping & Vertical Live Checklist

- **Clipping (`MusubiClip` / `IndClip`):** Start with 1–2 active campaigns, hook in first 2 seconds, readable bold captions, never bot views.
- **Vertical 9:16 Live Streams:** Great for farming fast subs/watch hours via the Shorts feed, **NEVER put your primary personal bank UPI QR directly on screen** (Indian cyber-cell P2P fraud freezes happen if compromised funds touch your personal account). Use a proper merchant/intermediary tip tool or funnel traffic to your `/ugc` or studio link instead.
